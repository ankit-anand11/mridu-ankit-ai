# Designer Survey — Carbon Adoption & Design-to-Handoff Quality

## Survey overview

**Purpose:** Quantify the frequency, severity, and distribution of Carbon adoption
challenges and handoff gaps across IBM product designers. Runs in parallel with or
immediately after the designer group session.

**Audience:** IBM product designers actively using Carbon, who have shipped at least
one Carbon-based feature in the last 6 months.

**Estimated time:** 6–8 minutes

**Format:** Quantitative — grids, multi-select, single select, and one optional
open-text field.
